Care that starts with you

Every person is different. We believe support should respect what matters to you, help you feel heard and encourage independence in everyday life.

We work with people living in {{ site.service_area }}, and with the families, representatives and professionals who support them. Our role is to listen first, explain clearly and agree support together.

We take account of each person’s communication preferences, culture, strengths and goals, and we keep talking as needs change.

Any support we provide is based on an individual assessment and agreed arrangements. An enquiry is the start of a conversation, not a care booking.

How we work with you

Clear expectations help everyone feel confident about support at home.

We will

  • {% icon "check" %}Take time to understand your preferences and routines
  • {% icon "check" %}Explain choices and next steps in clear language
  • {% icon "check" %}Encourage you to do the things you want to do yourself
  • {% icon "check" %}Respect your privacy, culture and identity
  • {% icon "check" %}Tell you when needs or circumstances change

We won’t

  • {% icon "x" %}Make decisions about your support without you
  • {% icon "x" %}Take over tasks you would rather do yourself
  • {% icon "x" %}Agree arrangements before an assessment
  • {% icon "x" %}Share your information without a lawful reason
