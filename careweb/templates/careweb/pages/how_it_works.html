Four steps, at your pace

You can ask questions at any point, and involve family or a representative if you would like to.

  1. Talk with us

    Tell us what you are looking for, ask questions and share the best way to contact you.

  2. Understand your needs

    We discuss routines, preferences, goals and any relevant considerations for safe support.

  3. Agree next steps

    If the service is suitable and capacity is available, the proposed plan and arrangements are discussed with you.

  4. Review together

    Once support begins, we keep talking and review the plan when needs or circumstances change.

What to have ready for the first conversation

You do not need everything below. It simply helps us understand what might be right for you.

  • {% icon "check" %}The area or postcode where support would be needed
  • {% icon "check" %}The kind of help you are looking for and when
  • {% icon "check" %}Routines, preferences and things that matter to you
  • {% icon "check" %}Who else you would like involved, such as family

Please do not include detailed medical information in the online enquiry form. We will discuss anything relevant with you directly.

Who can make an enquiry?

  • {% icon "heart" %}

    You

  • {% icon "users" %}

    Family or a representative

    Where possible, we involve the person who would receive support.

  • {% icon "clipboard" %}

    Professionals

    Health and social care professionals can make a referral.
